How to read this report

MAE (mean absolute error)
The average size of the forecast miss, in ug/m3 of PM2.5, 24 hours ahead. Lower is better. All numbers here are +24 h unless a label says otherwise.
Persistence and the smoother
Persistence predicts 'tomorrow equals now'. The smoother is the current simple baseline: a blend of the latest reading and the 24 h average (KAN-76). A model has to beat both to be worth building on.
Skill vs persistence
The percentage by which a model's MAE is below persistence's. 0% means no better than persistence. The smoother already earns about 10%, so a model must beat that, not only persistence.
Paired CI (95%, 72 h blocks)
For the same station-hours we subtract the smoother's error from the model's error and average. Negative means the model is better. The 95% confidence interval is the plausible range for that average, from a block bootstrap (72 h blocks, because neighbouring hours are correlated).
Why the CI must be entirely below zero
If the interval includes zero, the data cannot tell the model from the smoother: the gap may be noise. Only an interval completely below zero is evidence of an improvement. A negative point estimate alone is not.
Dev vs holdout
Dev is the period used to build and compare models; picking the best of many variants on it flatters the winner. The holdout is a locked later period scored once for the final candidate. These reports show dev only, so nothing here is evidence of future or holdout performance.
Fragile
The 72 h CI is below zero but the interval flips to include zero when the block length is changed to 24 h or 168 h. The conclusion depends on an analysis choice, so it is not trusted.
Exploratory / unadjusted
When many variants are tried, some look good by chance (at 95%, about 1 in 20). Intervals here are not corrected for that, so a lone 'success' among many is a lead to test on the holdout, not a result.

Written analysis

From docs/experiments/e12-gbm.md, which stays the written source of record.

E12 Gradient boosting: pooled LightGBM (KAN-89)

Verdict: NO WIN. No LightGBM variant beats the smoother; all 12 CIs include zero (dev only).

Question: does a tuned, pooled LightGBM on the v2 feature sets (fs_v2_core, fs_v2_full) beat persistence and the smoother at +24 h on the dev folds? Dev split only (5 weekly folds, 5 stations, 4200 pairs per horizon); the holdout was never scored (--holdout not used). Snapshot e02-v1 (sha256 a00cfc9e...), air4thai era, available_at = ts + 1 h (protocol section 2).

Result in one paragraph

Nothing beats the smoother. The best variant (e12-full-l2-delta, mae_24h 3.868) ties it: diff vs smoother +0.001 ug/m3, 95% CI [-0.23, +0.26], Diebold-Mariano p = 0.996. All 12 primary variants have a CI vs the smoother that straddles zero, and all 12 have a positive point estimate (the best is a tie at +0.001). Against persistence the models are clearly better (best: skill +0.103, diff -0.444, CI [-0.74, -0.18]), but so is the smoother (skill +0.103), so this is the smoother's gain, not the model's. The decision rule (CI high < 0 for both baselines and diff vs smoother <= -0.15) is not met by any variant. The 16 dev CIs are unadjusted; a correction would only widen them, so it cannot change this conclusion.

Design

  • Model factory: src/pm25_forecast/experiments/gbm.py (pm25_forecast.experiments.gbm:fit). One LightGBM per horizon (6, 12, 24 h), pooled over stations, station as a categorical column. All features from features/library_v2.py (prepare_series once per fold, build_features_v2); the model file only selects columns. Features are NaN-filled by LightGBM (nothing imputed); no forecast when there is no reading in [t-3h, t-1h].
  • Target: level (value at t+h) or delta (value minus the newest reading in [t-3h, t-1h], the persistence anchor; the prediction adds the anchor back).
  • Objectives: L2, L1, Huber. The Huber alpha was fixed at 8 ug/m3 and not tuned (a different alpha could move the Huber rows).
  • Tuning inside each fold's training part only: fixed budget of 8 configurations (the default plus 7 seeded random draws from a 4x3x3x3x3x3 grid: num_leaves, learning_rate, n_estimators, min_child_samples, feature_fraction, lambda_l2), scored by MAE on the level scale in 3 expanding-window inner folds, purged by the horizon plus the 1 h availability delay (a training label with target t is used only if t + 1 h <= validation target - h; the runs reported here were made with a purge that was 1 h too short, t <= validation target - h, see Caveats). The chosen configuration, all inner scores and the gain importance of every fit are in out/kan89/<prereg id>.tuning.jsonl (git-ignored, regenerated by the runs; the aggregate is printed below).
  • Cross-station group: the runner's predictor sees one station per call, so peers come from a memo of earlier snapshots. To make the result independent of the station order within an origin, bkk_mean and dev_from_bkk_mean are evaluated at origin - 1 h (training and prediction alike). nearest_station_pm25 is not used (no station coordinates in the snapshot). So "cross-station" below is a 1 h staler version of the library group.
  • Groups for the ablation: lags (pm25_lag_*, pm25_diff_*, missing_lag_*), rolling (rolling stats, pm25_mean_*, EWMA, n_valid_*), calendar, cross.
  • Seed 20261101 everywhere; LightGBM deterministic=True, 4 threads.

Variants and multiple comparisons

16 dev runs: 12 primary (2 feature sets x 3 objectives x 2 targets, preregistered in docs/experiments/prereg/e12-*.yaml before any run) and 4 ablations of the best (preregistered after the primary runs, before they ran). Selection rule fixed in advance: lowest dev mae_24h among the primary 12. One unlogged smoke run of the default configuration (fs_v2_core, L2, level, budget 8) on dev was made to check the pipeline before the preregistrations; it was not used for any choice. Dev CIs (95%, 72 h blocks) are not adjusted for the 16 runs; the best-of-12 selection is optimistically biased, and it still does not beat the smoother. No holdout finalist is proposed.

e12-full-l2-delta (MLflow run 76298486bbca4bc2b88987b3c742bd64): fs_v2_full (minus the nearest-station feature), objective L2, target delta, pooled over stations, 8-configuration search per fold and horizon. Chosen configurations at +24 h across its five folds: 3x {num_leaves 4, lr 0.1, 100 trees, min_child 20, ff 1.0, lambda 0}, 2x {num_leaves 7, lr 0.06, 100 trees, min_child 20, ff 0.8, lambda 1} (small, shallow models: 4 to 7 leaves, 100 trees). mae_24h 3.868 (skill vs persistence +0.103), diff vs persistence -0.444 [-0.74, -0.18], diff vs smoother +0.001 [-0.23, +0.26]; the 24 h and 168 h block sensitivities also straddle zero; negative vs the smoother in 3 of 5 folds (folds 1, 2, 4), +0.51 in fold 0. Caveats

  • Cross-station memo: when a station is skipped at an origin (no target or no baseline), its memo is not updated and stays stale, so the cross features served at prediction time can differ slightly from the training ones. A test shows that the order of stations within an origin does not change predictions when every station is called at every origin.
  • Purge: the inner-CV purge in the reported runs omitted the 1 h availability delay (about one hour of targets per station and inner fold was usable that was not yet available at the validation origin). The code now purges by h + 1 h; the numbers above were not re-run and the tuning choices could differ slightly, the effect on the outer-fold MAE is expected to be small but unmeasured.
  • The ablation preregistrations were edited after the runs (expected effect corrected to match the hypothesis: metric diff_full_minus_ablation_mae_24h, magnitude -0.05 = the decision threshold); the runs and their hypotheses were not changed.
  • All 16 runs score the same rows: n_pairs is 4200 at 6, 12 and 24 h in every run (MLflow store), so the comparisons are paired on identical pairs.
  • Dev only: 5 weekly folds, 5 stations, about 3 months of one season; the folds are highly autocorrelated and station series are correlated, so the effective sample is small. A CI that includes zero here means "no detectable difference", not "equal".
  • The smoother has one tuned parameter (beta, KAN-76) chosen on earlier data; the GBM has a budget of 8 configurations per fit. Neither is tuned on the dev folds scored here.
  • Peers are available to the cross-station group only through the memo and with a 1 h lag; a runner that passes all stations of an origin to the predictor would test the full library group.
  • Spike metrics are undefined (fewer than 50 observed spikes).
  • All runs clean (git_dirty false), valid, coverage 100%, no clipped or missing predictions (checked for the best run).

Recommendation

Do not promote any E12 variant and do not spend a holdout slot on one. If boosting is revisited, the likely gains are not in tuning but in new information (weather, CAMS, see E10/E11) and in a longer history (more weeks of data); the delta target and the L2 objective are the right defaults.
