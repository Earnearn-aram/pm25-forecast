How to read this report

MAE (mean absolute error)
The average size of the forecast miss, in ug/m3 of PM2.5, 24 hours ahead. Lower is better. All numbers here are +24 h unless a label says otherwise.
Persistence and the smoother
Persistence predicts 'tomorrow equals now'. The smoother is the current simple baseline: a blend of the latest reading and the 24 h average (KAN-76). A model has to beat both to be worth building on.
Skill vs persistence
The percentage by which a model's MAE is below persistence's. 0% means no better than persistence. The smoother already earns about 10%, so a model must beat that, not only persistence.
Paired CI (95%, 72 h blocks)
For the same station-hours we subtract the smoother's error from the model's error and average. Negative means the model is better. The 95% confidence interval is the plausible range for that average, from a block bootstrap (72 h blocks, because neighbouring hours are correlated).
Why the CI must be entirely below zero
If the interval includes zero, the data cannot tell the model from the smoother: the gap may be noise. Only an interval completely below zero is evidence of an improvement. A negative point estimate alone is not.
Dev vs holdout
Dev is the period used to build and compare models; picking the best of many variants on it flatters the winner. The holdout is a locked later period scored once for the final candidate. These reports show dev only, so nothing here is evidence of future or holdout performance.
Fragile
The 72 h CI is below zero but the interval flips to include zero when the block length is changed to 24 h or 168 h. The conclusion depends on an analysis choice, so it is not trusted.
Exploratory / unadjusted
When many variants are tried, some look good by chance (at 95%, about 1 in 20). Intervals here are not corrected for that, so a lone 'success' among many is a lead to test on the holdout, not a result.

Hypothesis and decision rule

Hypotheses (fixed before the runs)

  • Matched no-weather twin of the weather model: same rows, same family, same features minus weather; reference for the weather increment. [e11-ridge-base, e11-lgbm-base]
  • Adding Open-Meteo previous_day1 ECMWF IFS forecast features (wind speed and direction, humidity, temperature, rain, pressure, cloud, shortwave radiation) lowers +24 h dev MAE of this model versus its no-weather twin on identical rows. [e11-ridge-wx-d2, e11-ridge-wx, e11-lgbm-wx-d2, e11-lgbm-wx]

Decision rule

  • Dev exploration, nothing goes to the holdout from this ticket. Primary comparison: the MAE increment of the weather model over its matched no-weather model (same family, same rows), +24 h, 72 h block CI, 95% shown and 97.5% (Bonferroni over the 2 families) used for the decision. KAN-75 success rule applied to each weather model: (A) 95% CI of weather minus smoother MAE entirely below 0 AND MAE gain vs persistence >= 15%, or (B) onset catch share above the matched no-weather model by >= 10 points with paired CI above 0 and false-alarm share rising <= 2 points (judged only with >= 30 onsets on >= 8 days). GO if for at least one family (A or B) holds AND the increment CI at 97.5% is entirely below 0. PIVOT if not go but the increment point estimate is negative in at least one family. STOP otherwise. Sensitivity (descriptive, not decisive): previous_day2 weather (strictly available at the origin). [e11-ridge-base, e11-ridge-wx-d2, e11-ridge-wx, e11-lgbm-wx-d2 and 2 more]

E11: weather-forecast features (KAN-88)

Verdict: PIVOT. Weather forecasts do not lift any model past the smoother or past 15 % over persistence, so the KAN-75 success rule is not met. They do help LightGBM (about 0.31 ug/m3 lower +24 h MAE; CI below 0 overall, negative in all 5 folds) but not the linear model, and the helped LightGBM is still no better than the smoother. No live-ingestion ticket is written (it is for "go" only).

1. Question, hypothesis, decision rule (fixed before the runs)

Preregs: docs/experiments/prereg/e11-*.yaml (6 files, committed in c87259d before any logged run). Question: do lead-matched NWP forecast features lower +24 h MAE of LightGBM and the best linear model on identical rows, and does that meet the KAN-75 success rule? Rule (A) 95 % CI of weather minus smoother MAE entirely below 0 and MAE gain versus persistence at least 15 %, or (B) spike-onset catch share at least 10 points above the no-weather twin with paired CI above 0 and false alarms up at most 2 points (judged with at least 30 onsets on at least 8 days). Go = (A or B) for a family and the weather increment over its no-weather twin has a 97.5 % CI (Bonferroni over the 2 families) entirely below 0. Pivot = not go but a negative increment point estimate in a family. Stop = otherwise. Dev split only; the holdout was never touched.

2. Setup

  • Snapshot e02-v1 (hash a00cfc9e...), dev split, 5 weekly folds, weekly refit, 5 stations, 4,200 station-hours at +24 h on 840 hourly origins; air4thai era, targets before 2026-10-26. available_at = ts + 1 h assumption of protocol section 2 applies.
  • Base features: fs_v2_core (lags, rolling means, diffs, calendar). Weather: add-on set wx_v1 in src/pm25_forecast/features/weather.py (12 features at the target hour: wind speed, wind direction as sin/cos, humidity, temperature, rain, surface pressure, cloud cover, shortwave radiation, plus 24 h rain sum, wind-speed mean and shortwave sum).
  • Weather source: Open-Meteo Previous Runs API, ECMWF IFS 0.25 deg only, *_previous_day1 at the station coordinates; cache in ~/pm25-experiments/cache/weather (outside git, 5 files, request URLs in requests.jsonl). No ERA5 or analysis values. Sensitivity: *_previous_day2.
  • Boundary-layer height was requested but the API returns null for every hour (also for GFS and the other ECMWF model), so it is not a feature. Shortwave radiation stands in as the mixing driver. Wind direction is encoded as sin/cos.
  • Grid: the 5 stations fall on 3 distinct 0.25 deg cells (02t, 59t, 61t share one; bkp100t and bkp101t share one), so station weather is nearly collinear.
  • Models: ridge (alpha 30, standardised, mean-filled) and LightGBM (l1, 7 leaves, 150 rounds, lr 0.05, min child 50, mean of 5 seeds), one model per station and horizon, hyper-parameters frozen in the prereg, no tuning. "Best linear model": KAN-83/84 are not on main, so this is ridge on fs_v2_core, chosen in advance, not the best of a search. Swap it in if KAN-84 differs.
  • Identical rows: every variant, with and without weather, returns no forecast when the weather inputs of the target hour are missing; all variants scored on the same 4,200 pairs.
  • Serving-lag: a weather value for hour u with lead L days is used at origin t only if u - 24 h x L <= t (tested). Caveat: a real run is published hours after init, so previous_day1 at the +24 h target hour (nominal issue time = origin) is a slightly optimistic stand-in for what is live at t. previous_day2 (issued 24 h before the origin) is strictly available.

4. Reading

  • Ridge: weather adds nothing (+0.05 ug/m3, CI spans 0); the no-weather ridge is already as good as the smoother (-0.03, CI spans 0).
  • LightGBM: weather lowers MAE by 0.31 (CI [-0.46, -0.16], also at 97.5 %), negative in all 5 folds and with previous_day2. But the no-weather LightGBM is the worst model here (worse than the smoother by 0.42), and with weather it is still above the smoother (+0.10, CI spans 0). Weather repairs part of a weak model; it does not create a model that beats the baselines.
  • Not a causal claim. With three grid cells and only about nine weeks (one monsoon-to-dry-season transition), slow-moving weather features can act as a date or regime proxy, which a per-station tree model could exploit without any real weather effect. A longer history would separate those.
  • Spike onsets: 64 on 13 days; catch rates are 6-13 % for every model, so spike detection is not improved in any usable way (rule B not met; ridge +4.7 points is far from the 10-point bar).
  • Dev CIs are unadjusted for the 6 runs; this is exploratory and no holdout evidence.

5. Decision

Pivot. Do not build live ingestion of previous-run forecasts now. The condition to revisit is a longer history (the Previous Runs archive reaches back to 2024; a backfill of the PM2.5 side would be needed to use it) so that the LightGBM gain can be tested against a season-aware baseline. If KAN-89 (LightGBM on fs_v2_*) reaches a strong no-weather LightGBM, re-test the weather increment on top of it with kan88_analyze before any ingestion work.
